## 3. Regulatory and ecosystem evidence to retain

- **National Payment Systems Act 2022:** section 6 states that no person may provide payment services or operate a system unless duly licensed by the Central Bank, subject to the Act's provisions and exemptions. Read the Act and obtain local legal advice on the precise Osusu model: https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng%402022-06-23
- **Bank of Sierra Leone Banking Supervision:** regulator's public description of its licensing, regulation and supervision role: https://bsl.gov.sl/Banking_Supervision.html
- **National instant-payment service:** Ministry of Information and Civic Education reported on 13 February 2025 that seven banks and two mobile network operators had connected at that time. This is dated ecosystem context, not confirmation of current membership or Osusu access: https://moice.gov.sl/sierra-leone-launches-instant-payment-service/
- **PAPSS network coverage, June 2026:** Sierra Leone section lists Access, Bloom, Ecobank, FBNBank, GT Bank, Rokel Bank, SLCB, UBA and Vista Bank as participant send/receive channels: https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-3.pdf

## 4. Outreach sequence

1. Prepare a short Osusu institutional overview and a one-page proposed partner model.
2. Contact each bank through its official corporate/business channel and request the Head of Transaction Banking, Digital Banking, Partnerships or Corporate Banking.
3. Ask for a 30-minute exploratory meeting. Do not send customer data, secrets, production credentials or confidential architecture.
4. Send the capability questionnaire after the institution confirms the appropriate contact.
5. Request licence evidence and product/operating documentation before discussing launch claims.
6. Record answers and evidence in this tracker; mark unsupported statements as unverified.
7. Select a preferred partner only after legal, compliance, technical, operational and commercial review.
8. Obtain local legal advice and any required BSL engagement/approval before signing or launching regulated activities.

## 6. First-meeting scorecard

Score each item 0 (not evidenced), 1 (partial), or 2 (verified). Do not use the score as a substitute for legal approval.

| Dimension | What good evidence looks like | Score |
|---|---|---:|
| Licence and scope | Current regulator-confirmed authorisation covers the proposed activity | 0–2 |
| Funds custody / safeguarding | Written legal structure, account ownership and insolvency treatment | 0–2 |
| Savings product | Approved product terms, SLE/USD availability, rate and disclosure governance | 0–2 |
| Payments | Supported rails, settlement, reversals, limits and dispute handling | 0–2 |
| Technology | API documentation, sandbox, signed webhooks, idempotency and support | 0–2 |
| Compliance | KYC/AML responsibility matrix, monitoring and escalation | 0–2 |
| Security and privacy | Security controls, incident reporting, data roles and audit rights | 0–2 |
| Commercial fit | Fees, minimums, reserves, revenue model and termination terms | 0–2 |

**Hard stop:** do not proceed to live-money design or customer marketing if licence scope, custody/safeguarding, compliance responsibility or required regulatory approval is unresolved.

## 7. Current Osusu restrictions remain in force

- No interest rate, yield or return is configured or advertised.
- No customer funds are held or pooled by Osusu.
- No live deposits, withdrawals, automatic collection or Circle payouts.
- No claims that balances are insured or protected unless product/institution eligibility is confirmed in writing.
- Any prototype balances and transactions are test/demo data only.
